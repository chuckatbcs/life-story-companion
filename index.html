How it works

Conversation becomes a usable life story.

The Companion helps you stay with the person and the story while keeping the larger project organized behind the scenes. It is designed to reduce clerical friction without turning a meaningful interview into a checklist.

Write

Shape source-bounded material into a lasting life story without losing the person's voice.

What stays organized

The details that usually get lost between sessions.

Long-form family stories rarely fail because there were not enough questions. They fail because chronology, names, evidence, contradictions, and unfinished threads become hard to manage. The Companion is built around that problem.

People

Who is who

Keep relatives, friends, coworkers, nicknames, and relationships connected across the story.

Chronology

What happened when

Maintain a working timeline without forcing every memory into perfect order too early.

Places

Where life happened

Track homes, schools, towns, workplaces, trips, and other places that anchor memories.

Sources

What supports the memory

Keep photographs, records, interviews, documents, and family recollections tied to the claims they support.

Open loops

What still needs an answer

Preserve unanswered questions and parked threads so they can be revisited instead of forgotten.

Themes

What keeps recurring

Notice patterns such as work, family, home, responsibility, migration, faith, loss, or reinvention without over-writing the story.

Why a skill

Keep the assistant you already use. Add a method built for life stories.

The Life Story Companion is not another chatbot or standalone AI assistant. It is a separately licensed skill designed to run inside a compatible AI assistant and give that assistant a structured way to help with a family-history project.

Human first

The conversation remains the source. The skill helps organize, question, compare, and draft from the material you provide rather than inventing a cleaner story than the evidence supports.

Project aware

It remembers the shape of the work. Chronology, source provenance, coverage, contradictions, and unfinished threads remain part of the project instead of disappearing between chats.

Compatibility without lock-in

Supported assistants will be listed only after they are tested.

The Companion core is intentionally vendor-neutral. Each platform adapter must prove that it can preserve the important parts of a life-story project before that platform is presented here as supported.

Evidence, privacy, and control

The story stays human. The uncertainty stays visible.

The Companion is designed to help organize evidence without quietly converting guesses into facts or private material into public material.

Memory is not a court record

Remembered experience, family lore, documented evidence, inference, and unresolved claims remain distinguishable instead of being flattened into one false level of certainty.

Raw sources stay separate

Recordings, transcripts, photographs, and documents remain distinct from summaries and generated prose so later drafts can be checked against the original material.

Sharing requires a choice

The method treats research, publishing, external sharing, and visibility changes as separate actions that require appropriate authorization rather than automatic next steps.

Questions

What the Companion is — and what it is not.

Is this a separate AI assistant?

No. The Companion is an AI skill designed for use inside a compatible AI assistant.

Does buying the handbook include the skill?

No. The handbook is complete on its own. The Companion is a separate digital product with its own availability and licensing.

Does the skill write the story for me?

Its job is to help you capture, organize, verify, and shape source material. The human story, evidence, and final editorial choices remain central.

Can it work for someone other than Dad?

Yes. The method is being kept family-role neutral so the same Companion can support different relationships and future legacy-book editions.

Which AI assistants will it support?

Only tested adapters will be listed as supported. Exact compatibility, installation steps, and platform limitations will be published here when release testing is complete.

Will my project be trapped in one platform?

The core is being designed around exportable structured project state and explicit source references. Each platform's actual export limits will still be documented before release.
