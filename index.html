The Life Story Companion

A context-aware AI companion designed to help families interview, organize, verify, and preserve a life story without turning the conversation into a rigid questionnaire.

Built for real conversation

The interviewer follows what the storyteller actually says, asks one useful question at a time, and returns to the larger life-story structure without losing productive tangents.

Designed for long projects

Recordings and transcripts remain source evidence while compact timelines, people, places, open questions, and coverage records keep later sessions coherent.

Separate from the book

The Life Story Handbook stands on its own. The Companion is a separately licensed digital product for readers who want the method implemented as an AI workflow.

Vendor-neutral direction

The core method is being designed so future supported AI platforms and distribution channels can change without changing the printed handbook.
