Privacy

Private family stories deserve clear boundaries.

This page describes the current pre-release privacy posture of The Life Story Companion. Platform-specific disclosures will be updated before any customer-facing release.

Current status: pre-release. No public Life Story Companion purchase, customer account system, public proprietary download, or production Companion-hosted MCP service is live yet.

What the public website collects

The public availability website is informational. It does not currently provide a form for uploading interviews, recordings, family documents, photographs, payment information, or Life Story Companion project data.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting and browser/network infrastructure may process ordinary technical request data according to their own policies.

What the Companion is designed to handle

A life-story project may include names, relationships, dates, photographs, recordings, transcripts, documents, private memories, and other personal or family information. Because those materials can be sensitive, the Companion is designed around explicit visibility labels, source provenance, exportability, and narrow data-use claims.

Platform data boundaries

The Life Story Companion is an AI skill used inside a compatible AI assistant. Data handling therefore depends in part on the specific host platform, adapter, storage configuration, and features the user enables.

Before a platform is listed as supported, its compatibility documentation must disclose what the host stores, what any Companion service stores, what remains in the user's own files, what data may leave the user's environment, and how export or deletion works where supported.

Companion-managed durable state

An internal persistence prototype exists for development, but it is not a public production service. No public claim of Companion-managed cross-session storage will be made until the exact storage, transport, authentication, backup, recovery, retention, export, and deletion behavior has been tested and documented.

Visibility and control

The Companion method uses the concepts PRIVATE, FAMILY, PUBLIC, and RESTRICTED_UNTIL when the host permits them. The skill is designed not to publish, externally share, change visibility, or conduct external research without appropriate user authorization.

Raw sources and generated material

The Companion is designed to keep recordings, transcripts, photographs, and documents conceptually separate from summaries and generated prose. A summary is not treated as a replacement for the raw source.

Payments and licensing

No payment or customer entitlement system is live yet. If commercial distribution is activated later, this page will be updated before production use to describe any customer-account, purchase, licensing, and service-provider data flows that apply.

Changes to this notice

This pre-release notice may change as supported platforms and distribution methods are finalized. Material changes will be reflected at this permanent public destination before the associated production capability is represented as available.
