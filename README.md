# Life Story Companion

Public landing page and availability hub for **The Life Story Companion**, the separately distributed digital companion to *The Life Story Handbook*.

## Purpose

This repository is intentionally public and contains only material safe to publish: product information, availability notices, documentation, compatibility status, support information, and routing links to official purchase/install channels.

The proprietary interview engine, prompt logic, memory architecture, ingestion logic, paid packages, licensing implementation, and paywall source are **not** stored here.

## Permanent destination

GitHub Pages is intended to publish this repository at:

`https://chuckatbcs.github.io/life-story-companion/`

That address is designed to be safe to print in the paperback and encode in its QR code. Marketplace URLs, prices, supported platforms, and purchase links can change behind this page without changing the printed book.

## Product relationship

- **The Life Story Handbook** — standalone paperback methodology.
- **Life Story Companion** — separately licensed AI skill used inside a compatible AI assistant.
- **life-story-companion-premium** — private source repository for proprietary/paywalled product code and packaging.

## Compatibility policy

Do not advertise a platform as supported merely because the core instructions can be pasted into it.

A public compatibility claim should follow tested adapter work covering, as applicable:

- installation and update path;
- durable project state;
- source/file handling;
- transcript ingestion;
- provenance and source references;
- export/portability;
- recovery behavior;
- known platform limitations;
- data-boundary disclosure.

Until a platform adapter passes that review, the public site should describe the product as **pre-release** and avoid unsupported compatibility claims.

Public compatibility page:

`https://chuckatbcs.github.io/life-story-companion/compatibility.html`

Source/status detail: [`COMPATIBILITY.md`](COMPATIBILITY.md).

## Brand and future-edition flexibility

Keep the Companion and its public website **family-role neutral**. The current published journal began with Dad, but future legacy products may extend to Mom, Grandpa, Grandma, children, or other family relationships.

Do not hard-code public branding, navigation, permanent URLs, or the Companion's core product language around one family role unless a deliberately separate edition requires it. The shared system should remain reusable across a broader family-legacy series without forcing a rebrand.
